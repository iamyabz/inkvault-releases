# InkVault 0.8.4

**Sharp ink when you zoom in, and tape and stickies in both readers.**

The ink was soft for a reason I had wrong twice: zooming into a page magnified the pen layer instead of redrawing it at the size you were actually looking at. It now redraws at the zoom you are using.

**Markup works on every page again.** The attempts at the sharpness problem left pages with nothing on them without a drawing layer at all, and iOS only asks for one once — so the pen worked on some pages and not others.

**Tape and sticky notes are now in the reader beside your notes too**, not only in full screen. That reader had no button and nothing behind it, which is why they never appeared there at all.
