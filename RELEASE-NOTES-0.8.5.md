# InkVault 0.8.5

**The reader stops freezing, and marking up now lives in one place.**

Opening a long book left the app frozen for six to seven seconds at a time — every return from full screen re-read the entire book's text on the main thread. Davidsons is 6.2 million characters. That work now happens once, in the background.

**Marking up is a full-screen activity.** The pen, tape and sticky notes have been taken out of the reader beside your notes. Having both readers carry them meant two of everything behind them, including two separate stores for tape and stickies over the same file — so a strip added in one place was invisible in the other.

**Ink is drawn at the size you are actually looking at.** Zooming in no longer magnifies the pen layer.

**A sticky note added to a page now opens as a card**, rather than appearing only as a small tab in the margin.
