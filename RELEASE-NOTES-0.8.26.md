# InkVault 0.8.26

**Big scanned textbooks now get a table of contents — and the Mac has one at last.**

Davidson's had no contents at all: the publisher's outline was gone, its printed contents page scans as two jumbled columns, and the text-scanning path produces no headings. Three dead ends.

So the app now reads the chapter title the book prints at the top of every page. Where that title changes is where the chapter changes — nothing to parse, no page numbers to trust. On Davidson's that recovers **all 28 chapters** with the right starting pages, with no scanning and no cost.

**The Mac reader has a Contents button.** It was on iPad only, which meant the machine with the most room to show it was the one where a 1400-page book could only be navigated by scrolling. It highlights the chapter you are currently in.
