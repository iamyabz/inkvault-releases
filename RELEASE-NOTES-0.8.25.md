# InkVault 0.8.25

**When you ask about a page you have written on, the AI is now actually told which words are yours.**

Your handwriting was already being sent to the AI separately from the page's own text, and clearly labelled. But the instruction explaining what that label means had only been added to one of the two ways of asking a question — so when you asked about a page, or across your documents, the model saw a label nobody had explained and had to guess.

Both now carry the rule: your notes are never presented as the document's words, the answer says which is which, and a note that contradicts the page is pointed out rather than quietly ignored.
