# InkVault 0.8.11

**The AI can now tell the textbook from your own notes on it.**

Your handwriting on a PDF has been read for a while, but it was folded into the page's own text — so when you asked a question, the model saw the book and your margin notes as one indistinguishable block. It could not say which was which, because it was never told there were two.

They are separate now. Ask about a page you have annotated and the answer can say what the page says, what you wrote on it, and where the two disagree — citing each properly.

Existing books are converted automatically the next time the app opens, so notes you have already made are included.

Finding a page *because* of what you wrote on it, rather than what it says, is the next step.
