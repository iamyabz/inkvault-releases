# 0.9.1

## You can now rub out another device's handwriting

Marks made on the iPad can be erased from the iPhone, and the other way round.
Until now they could be added to but never removed: annotations travelled
between devices as a rendered picture of ink, so there was nothing to take away.

The strokes themselves now travel alongside that picture, so another device's
handwriting sits in your canvas like your own. The eraser just works on it —
there is no mode to enter and nothing new to learn.

Erasing is a request rather than a deletion, because no device is allowed to
write over another's work. The device that made the strokes removes them the
next time you open the document there. Every up-to-date device hides them
immediately; the Mac catches up once the device that owns them has been opened.
