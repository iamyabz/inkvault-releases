# 0.9.53

This update makes InkVault better to use, safer with your data, and clearer about what it does. It builds on the two fixes in 0.9.52.

## Copying an AI answer now pastes it cleanly

When you copy an answer from Ask AI, you now get readable text — headings, lists, tables and formulas laid out the way you saw them — instead of the raw markup. It works the same on Mac, iPhone and iPad.

## Search feels instant

As you type in search and the command bar, the matching pages now appear straight away, without the short pause there used to be, and without any extra work behind the scenes.

## A fix for a crash when using split view (Mac)

Opening InkVault beside another window in split view could close the app. It no longer does.

## Your saved AI keys are better protected (Mac)

The keys you enter for your AI provider are now kept in the Mac's protected Keychain instead of a scrambled file on disk. InkVault still never sends them to us — each one goes only to the company it belongs to — and moving them across happens by itself, once, the next time you open the app.

## Sharing a document shares less about you

A shared `.inkvault` file no longer carries your name, your computer's account name, or your devices' identifiers unless you choose to include them. Turning off "Include annotations" now leaves out everything you wrote — your notes, your highlights and the summaries — not just some of it. And InkVault now refuses a malformed or oversized shared file instead of struggling to open it.

## Clearer about what's on and off

The Research Chat screen now says plainly what the web-search switch does and where your question goes. And InkVault now shows the full acknowledgements for the open-source software it's built on.

If InkVault ever closes on you or behaves oddly, updating is always the first thing to try.
