# InkVault 0.8.0

**Tape and sticky notes work on PDFs now.**

Your handwritten notes have had tape and sticky notes from the start. Books and papers only ever had the pen — which meant the place you actually study was the one place you could not cover an answer to test yourself, or pin a card to a page.

Open any document, tap the new button beside the pen, and add a sticky note or a strip of tape. Drag either one where you want it. Tap tape to lift it and see what is underneath, tap it again to put it back. Tap a sticky's tab to open the card, and the chevron to put it away. Hold anything down to remove it.

The pen still works exactly as before, and the two share the page properly: with the pen out every touch draws, and with it put away a tap lands on the tape or the card you meant.
