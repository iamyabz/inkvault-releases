# InkVault 0.8.9

**The tape shape tool can be cancelled, and tells you when a shape was not accepted.**

Arming "Draw a tape shape" and then changing your mind used to leave it armed with no way to back out — and it stayed armed after you put the pen away, so a stroke drawn later could turn into tape unexpectedly. Tap the hint to cancel, or just put the pen away.

**A shape that is not accepted now says why.** Tape needs a closed outline: draw a box or a circle around what you want covered, ending roughly where you began. A swipe across a line is not a closed shape, and previously that failed silently.
