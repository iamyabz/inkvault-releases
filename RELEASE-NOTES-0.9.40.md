# 0.9.40

## Dragging a document into a folder works

It never did. The folder would light up as you dragged over it, you would let
go, and nothing happened — and with several documents selected the app could
die mid-drag. Both are fixed, and filing now works for a single document, for a
whole ⌘-selection, and for dragging one folder above another.

## Sharing goes through the share sheet

Choosing Share on a document or a folder now hands the file to AirDrop,
Messages, Notes, Shortcuts and everything else in the system share sheet,
instead of only offering to save it somewhere for you to go and find. Saving is
still there as the second button.

Before it sends, the panel tells you what you are about to share: whether your
annotations are included, the real size with duplicates counted once, and a
reminder that the original files travel with it. Large shares now show progress
rather than appearing to do nothing.

## iPhone and iPad

No changes in this release. 0.9.39's launch animation and 0.9.38's fixes for
shared files are unchanged.
