# InkVault 0.8.13

**What you draw on a textbook now reaches your other devices — and the AI reads what you wrote inside your sticky notes.**

Ink, tape and sticky notes you add to a PDF are published the same way a note's annotations already were, so they show up on your Mac and your other devices. Open the book on the iPad, mark it up, leave the reader, and it travels.

**Handwriting inside a sticky note is now read and indexed.** It was the one surface you could write on that nothing ever looked at — so the thing you most wanted to find later was the thing that could not be found. Ask a question and your cards count as your notes, kept separate from what the textbook says.

Nothing is re-read or re-uploaded unless it actually changed, so closing a book you only read costs nothing.
