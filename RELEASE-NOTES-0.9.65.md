# InkVault 0.9.65

## Harder quizzes (Mac, iPhone and iPad)

- **Choose a difficulty: Standard, Hard or Exam-hard.** Standard is the quiz
  you know. Hard and Exam-hard write longer, exam-style questions that need you
  to combine facts, with every option plausible and five choices instead of
  four. Every answer is still checked against your own pages.
- **Questions that link pages.** On Hard and Exam-hard, when you quiz on more
  than three pages, questions can draw on two places at once, the way real
  exams do.
- **Auto question count.** Turn on Auto and InkVault works out the key points
  in what you selected and writes the fewest questions that cover them all.
- **Go harder after a good score.** Score 80% or more and you can take a harder
  quiz focused on what you missed. Sit Again still gives you the same paper.

## Also

- In Crystal Glass, buttons like Take Quiz and Sit Again are now one clean
  glass pill.
