# InkVault 0.9.58

A handwriting release. Writing and drawing on iPad feel steadier, no stroke is
lost when the page is still moving, and pictures in a note move, resize and
turn the way you expect.

## Writing and drawing on iPad

- **No more lost strokes on a moving page.** Putting the Pencil down while the
  canvas is still gliding after a flick used to stop the scroll and drop the
  stroke. Now the page stops and the ink lands.
- **Less stutter at the start of each stroke.** The floating tool bar no longer
  sits in the Pencil's path, so the first moment of a stroke drops about two
  thirds fewer frames. The bar looks and works exactly as before.
- **Faster tool switching.** Changing pen, highlighter or eraser no longer
  makes the whole screen redraw.
- **Undo lights up straight away** after your first stroke.
- **Closing a large canvas** no longer causes a big memory spike.
- **Pinch-zooming a PDF** keeps a narrow page centred while you pinch, instead
  of jumping sideways when you let go.
- **The Zones button** moves clear of the tool bar wherever you dock it.

## Pictures in notes

With the **Move** tool:

- **One finger** moves a picture.
- **Two fingers** resize it with a pinch and turn it with a twist, whether your
  fingers are on the picture or beside it, so small pictures are easy to handle.
- **One finger on empty canvas** pans the page.
- Writing with the Pencil beside a picture never drags it.

## Also

- Text boxes keep their alignment on the Mac and in exported PDFs.
- Text boxes edited from a full-screen PDF are sized in their own font, so wide
  fonts are no longer clipped.
- A page's handwriting is only counted as read for search once it is safely
  saved, so a failed save can no longer drop it from search.

## Notes

The sync format is unchanged from 0.9.57, so your Mac and iPad stay in sync
whichever one updates first.

This is a beta build. Keep your own backups of anything you cannot afford to
lose, and do not use InkVault for clinical, legal or other professional
decisions.
