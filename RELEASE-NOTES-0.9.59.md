# InkVault 0.9.59

A new look for the Mac, a way out of any quiz, and the iPad tool bar fix.

## Crystal Glass (Mac)

A new, optional appearance for InkVault on the Mac. Turn it on in
**Settings ▸ Appearance ▸ Crystal Glass**. It needs macOS 26 or later and Dark
mode; in Light mode, or on an older Mac, InkVault stays in Classic.

- The window becomes smoked glass that lets your desktop show through.
- The search bar is polished crystal. Light runs along its edge when you click
  into it, starting from where you clicked.
- The selected item in the sidebar is a frosted glass piece, with a soft
  light when you move between sections.
- Study History, the reader, the media players and Settings all follow the
  same look.
- Your documents, handwriting, PDF pages and videos are never tinted.
- Reduce Transparency, Increase Contrast and Reduce Motion are respected.
- **Classic** is unchanged and is still the default. Switching between them is
  instant and keeps your place, your selection and your search.

## End a quiz whenever you like (Mac, iPhone and iPad)

You no longer have to go through every question to stop a test. **End Test** is
always there while you sit a quiz, and it shows how far you've got:

- **Continue Test** takes you back exactly where you were.
- **Submit Early** marks what you've answered. Unanswered questions count as
  wrong, and the result says it was submitted early.
- **Abandon Test** stops without saving a result. The quiz and all your earlier
  attempts stay, your best score doesn't change, and nothing is sent to the AI.

Closing the window or swiping the quiz away asks the same question, so an
unfinished attempt is never lost by accident.

## Study History

- On the Mac, the quiz list now uses the same glass as the rest of Crystal,
  with each section grouped together and your best scores in colour.
