# InkVault 0.8.1

**Resting your hand on the page no longer draws.**

Marking up took any touch, so a palm left a stroke. It is Apple Pencil only again. If you do not have a Pencil, there is a switch in Settings under "Marking up" to draw with a finger.

**Tape and sticky notes now appear, and markup works on every page.** These were the same problem: iOS only asks an app once whether a page needs an overlay, and InkVault was saying no to any page you had not already drawn on. Adding a sticky, or picking up the pen, then did nothing on the pages already in front of you and worked on ones you scrolled to afterwards.

Pages you are looking at now get the pen the moment you pick it up, and a sticky or a strip of tape appears where you put it.
