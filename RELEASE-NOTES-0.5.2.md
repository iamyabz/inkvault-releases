# InkVault 0.5.2

**You choose which files go to iCloud, and nothing is too large to sync any more.**

There used to be a 100 MB ceiling. Anything bigger stayed on the machine it was added to, quietly, and the only clue was a line buried in Settings. That is gone. A four-gigabyte textbook is exactly the case where being locked out of your own library matters most, and the storage being filled is your own, so the size of a file is your decision rather than the app's.

In its place, a switch on every document, in a new **Documents by size** list in Settings on Mac, iPhone and iPad. Search it, or show the whole library.

**The switch is only about the file itself.** Every document's text and search index sync always, whatever you choose. So a textbook you read on the Mac can stay on the Mac and still turn up when you search from your phone, and still be something you can ask questions about there. The phone simply says the file is not in iCloud, instead of pretending it is on its way. Turn the switch on and the book uploads; turn it off again and it comes back out of iCloud, so the space is genuinely returned.

Documents under 100 MB are on by default, as before. Larger ones wait to be asked for.

**Ask AI about the page you are reading**, on Mac and iPad. A panel beside the page on the Mac, a sheet on iPad, answering from that page only, so the answer cannot drift into the rest of the document. In a two-page spread, click or tap the side you mean.

**A two-page reader that reads like a book.** One spread at a time, no gutter down the middle, page one on its own as a cover. Swipe on iPad or use the arrows on the Mac.

**Also fixed**

- Clicking a document card in the library selected the wrong document unless you clicked exactly on its thumbnail.
- Renaming a document took most of a second and blocked the app while it re-encoded the entire library and rebuilt the whole search index. It now costs nothing noticeable.
- Handwritten notes open on the Mac with their tape and sticky notes working, and a note read full screen on iPad keeps them too.
