# InkVault 0.5.1

**Your handwritten notes now open on the Mac with their tape and sticky notes working.**

Until now a note reached the Mac as a flat page: whatever you had covered with tape stayed covered, and sticky notes did not come across at all. Now a note opens as itself. Click a strip of tape to peel it back and see what is underneath, click it again to stick it down. Sticky notes sit as tabs down the margin, and open and close with a click. You cannot write on a note here, which is the point: the Mac is for reading them.

What is peeled and what is open is remembered on this Mac, and it is never written back into the note. Nothing you do in the reader can change what you wrote.

**Two things were wrong with every note this app has ever published, and both are fixed.**

Tape was being drawn *underneath* the handwriting, so a published or printed note showed the writing painted on top of the strip that was meant to be hiding it. And a strip drew as a rounded pill whatever shape you actually chose, so a rectangle came out with rounded ends and an ellipse came out as a rectangle. Notes you publish from now on are correct. Notes published before this will be corrected the next time they are re-read on your iPad.

**Storage now tells the truth about what is using space.**

The storage list only ever counted downloaded originals, and reported one whole category — the originals stored on this Mac — as zero, because it was looking for them in a folder they have never been kept in. On a real library that was over a hundred megabytes missing from the total. A number that does not add up is what sends you hunting through Settings in the first place.

**Also in this release**

- Renaming a note now actually renames it everywhere. The new name reached the note but never the library, so it never travelled to your other devices and search kept showing the old one.
- A note that could not be read within its time limit now says so, instead of failing silently.
