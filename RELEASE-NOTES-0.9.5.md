# 0.9.5

## Sharing a marked-up PDF includes the pages you added

Flattening walked the book's own pages, so every blank sheet you inserted — and
everything written on one — was missing from the file you sent. Those are
usually the pages that were the reason for sending it. The export now follows
the order you read in.

## Text mode counts the book's pages, not yours

A sheet inserted after page 3 was listed as the last page of the book, and a
1,394-page textbook said "1395". Sheets now sit where you put them and are
labelled the way they are everywhere else — "412a · your sheet". Asking about
one works too; the pill used to go dead on the one page whose whole content is
your own writing.

## Jumping to a search hit on a sheet

A hit on your own handwriting landed on the last page of the book instead of on
the sheet. It was findable and then unreachable, which is the one thing making
it searchable was for.
