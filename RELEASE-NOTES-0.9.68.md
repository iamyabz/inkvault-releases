# InkVault 0.9.68

## Quizzes across several lectures (Mac, iPhone and iPad)

- **Questions that connect lectures.** Select several videos or documents and
  choose Hard or Exam-hard, and most questions now combine facts from two
  different lectures. For example, how tetanus and botulism toxins act on
  different neurons, or why infant botulism and C. difficile both depend on
  missing gut flora. This is the kind of question board exams ask.
- **Every lecture is read.** A big selection used to stop being read after
  about ten videos. Now every lecture you select reaches the quiz writer, and
  questions are shared across them.
- **Fewer failed quizzes.** If the hardest settings occasionally came back
  empty after a long wait, the quiz is now written again automatically
  instead of failing.
