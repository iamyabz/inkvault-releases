# InkVault 0.9.66

## Videos away from their drive (Mac)

- **Copy a video into InkVault when you import it.** The import sheet now asks
  where the video lives. Link to it where it is, as before, or copy it into
  InkVault so it plays anywhere, even without the drive it came from. The sheet
  shows how much space the copy takes, and what is free, before you choose.
- **Videos in iCloud now play when their drive is unplugged.** A video whose
  file isn't on this Mac now downloads from iCloud and opens by itself when it
  arrives, instead of saying "Video not found".
- **A clearer message when a video really isn't here.** It now names the drive
  to connect, instead of suggesting a re-import.

## Also

- **Settings ▸ Documents by size** shows where each video lives: copied into
  InkVault, or linked on a drive, and whether that drive is connected. Linked
  videos no longer vanish from the list when their drive is unplugged, and the
  total counts only what InkVault actually stores.
- **Emptying Recently Deleted no longer moves a linked video's original file**
  off your drive to the Trash. Videos copied into InkVault are still removed
  with their document.
