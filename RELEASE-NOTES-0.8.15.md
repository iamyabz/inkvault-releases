# InkVault 0.8.15

**Questions about big books are now answered from the right pages — and cost a fraction of what they did.**

When a document was too long to send to the AI whole, it was cut at the start: roughly the first 150 pages of a textbook, no matter what you asked. A question about page 900 was answered from the front matter, at full price.

Now the pages that actually mention your question are the ones sent. It is decided on your device from text the app already has, so it costs nothing, adds no delay, and works offline. In testing, a question that previously sent 3,000 characters of the wrong pages now sends 476 characters of the right one.

Pages you wrote on count for a little more, since they are usually the ones you were thinking about.
