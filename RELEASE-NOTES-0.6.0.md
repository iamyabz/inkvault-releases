# InkVault 0.6.0

**Getting around a long book.**

A textbook used to open on page one and offer you a scrollbar. Now there are three ways through it, and you don't have to choose between them.

**Contents.** InkVault reads the book's own table of contents — the one the publisher put there — and lets you jump straight to a chapter by name, or type a page number to go to it. Books with hundreds of index entries open at their chapters, with everything else a tap away. Scanned books have no built-in contents, so InkVault builds one from the headings it read while indexing, and tells you that's where it came from.

**All pages.** A grid of every page, for when you remember what something looked like but not where it was.

**A scrubber** along the bottom for moving through a book by feel.

**Ask AI is now a window.** It used to slide up from the bottom and cover the half of the page you were probably asking about. Drag it wherever you like, take the corner to resize it, or collapse it to its title bar — and it stays where you put it next time. Every answer has a Copy button, and long-pressing any message copies it, or the whole conversation.
