# InkVault 0.9.17

## The Mac can mark up a PDF

It could always show the tape and cards another device had put on a page, and
never make any. Now it can: lay a strip of tape, stick a card on the page and
type into it, drag, resize, recolour, delete.

- Drag a card by its top strip, click its body to type, resize from the corner.
- A tool makes one object and hands you straight back to Arrange with it
  selected, so the next thing you do is to the thing you just made.
- Arrange has its own toolbar button. Click bare page, or press Escape, when you
  are done.
- One tape palette across both platforms, so the same strip is the same colour
  whichever machine drew it.

Handwriting stays read-only on the Mac. macOS ships the drawing format but not
the canvas, so there is nothing to write with — the iPad's ink is drawn here,
faithfully, and is not editable.

Each device writes only what it made and reads everything. So a card made on the
Mac appears on the iPad and a card made on the iPad appears on the Mac, and
neither can overwrite the other's work.

## Cards from another device can be opened and read

A note made elsewhere used to arrive as a tab with a dot on it: you could see
that somebody had written something and never find out what. Tapping one opens
it now, on every device, and a strip of tape can be peeled wherever you are.

## Flashcard decks and quiz results follow you

Saved decks and completed sittings lived only on the machine that made them.
They sync now — make a deck on the iPad and it is on the Mac, sit a paper on the
phone and the score is waiting on the iPad, with the same cards, the same
citations and the same page provenance.

Deleting one deletes it everywhere, and it stays deleted: a device that has been
switched off for a year cannot bring it back when it returns.

## Ask AI and the reader are legible again

Answers, summaries and reader text were being drawn in black regardless of
theme, which in dark mode is black on a dark panel. So was every search
highlight, which was invisible in both themes. All of it now uses the same inks
the Mac's summaries have always used: violet section headings, teal
sub-headings, proper spacing and hanging indents.

- Ask AI on a page no longer opens on page 1 of the book.
- You can select part of an answer again, instead of only the whole thing.
- The Ask panel sits on a solid background rather than a blur of the page
  behind it, and its header can no longer be dragged off the top of the screen.

## Choosing pages to study

The page picker never said how to choose more than one. It does now: click a
page, then hold Shift and click another.
