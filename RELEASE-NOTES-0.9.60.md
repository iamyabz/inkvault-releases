# InkVault 0.9.60

A small fix for Crystal Glass on the Mac.

- **The selected sidebar item stays glass.** Clicking in the sidebar, or moving
  through it with the keyboard, used to turn the selected item into a solid
  block of your Mac's accent colour. It now keeps its frosted crystal look,
  whatever accent colour you use. Keyboard navigation and VoiceOver work as
  before, and Classic is unchanged.

Everything from 0.9.59 (Crystal Glass, End Test and Abandon Test, the Study
History refresh) is included.
