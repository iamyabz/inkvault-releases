# InkVault 0.8.17

**Reading your handwriting on a PDF now costs about a sixth of what it did.**

When you write on a page of a book, the app sends that writing to be read. It was sending the **whole page** as a large lossless image, even if you wrote two words in a corner — and at a size that changed depending on which device you drew on.

It now sends just the part you wrote on, at the same settings your notes have always used. For a typical margin note that is about **17%** of the image it was sending before, with the same words coming back.

This applies to every annotated page and to every sticky note you write in.
