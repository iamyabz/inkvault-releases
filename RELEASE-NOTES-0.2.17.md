## InkVault 0.2.17

### Search understands time
Your notes have always known when you added them; now the search box does too.

- **"on july i did so and so"**. Finds notes added in July that match the words.
- **"from january 2026 to june 2026 i wrote about epistaxis"**, a date range plus content.
- **"last week pathology"**, "yesterday", "this month", "in 2025", all work.
- **"in july"** on its own lists everything you added that month, newest first.

A calendar chip echoes the window as InkVault understood it ("July 2026"), so a misread is visible rather than a silent wrong filter. Ranking is untouched, a date filters, it never scores.

### Every note shows when you added it
Bottom-right of the card (Mac) and the row (iPhone/iPad). Recent notes say "2 hours ago"; older ones show the real date. In the same form the search box accepts, so the display teaches the query. Also fixed: the Mac was quietly showing the *modified* date, so re-scanning an old note made it look new.

### Bulk imports everywhere
Audio, video, and spreadsheets can now be selected many at a time on iPhone and iPad. The import queue works through them one by one. Documents already allowed it, and the Mac always has.
