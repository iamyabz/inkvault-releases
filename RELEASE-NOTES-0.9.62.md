# InkVault 0.9.62

- **Long recordings in Urdu, Arabic and other languages now finish
  transcribing.** A recording transcribed on your Mac could run all the way to
  the end and then fail with "Couldn't read Whisper's output", because a
  single letter was split in two in Whisper's results. InkVault now reads the
  transcript anyway, keeping every whole word.

Includes 0.9.61's live transcription progress, and Crystal Glass and End Test.
