# InkVault 0.6.2

**Ask AI can draw.**

When an answer is really a shape — a mechanism, a pathway, a cascade, a cycle, a branching decision — InkVault can now include a small diagram alongside the words. Boxes and arrows, drawn natively, so they stay sharp at any size and follow your text size and appearance settings. Most answers still won't have one, which is the point: a flowchart of three bullet points is harder to read than three bullet points.

**Four reader fixes, all reported.**

The contents list showed two chapters for a book that has forty. It was opening at the shallowest level of the outline, so a book beginning "Front matter / Volume 1 / Volume 2 / Index" hid every chapter underneath those four lines. It now shows as much of the outline as fits comfortably.

Leaving full screen on a long book could freeze for a moment — it was checking every page of the book for pen marks, one file at a time, even when there were none.

The page slider no longer fades away while you are using it.

Ask AI has moved up off the slider, so reaching for it doesn't send you across the book.
