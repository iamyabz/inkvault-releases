# 0.9.52

This version fixes two problems in InkVault for Mac. iPhone and iPad are not affected.

## An answer with maths in it no longer closes InkVault (Mac)

InkVault 0.9.51 began drawing the formulas in AI answers as real mathematics: fractions, roots and symbols, set properly. On most Macs, the first answer that contained a formula closed the app instead, because the fonts it draws with were left out of the download. They are now part of the app.

The problem happened while an answer was being drawn. It did not change your library, your notes or your settings. If InkVault closed on you while it was answering a question, updating fixes it.

Every Mac release is now checked for this before it is published: the finished app has to draw its formulas on a Mac that has never built InkVault.

## A heavily annotated book shows its annotations again (Mac)

Since 0.9.50, a book with a lot of annotations from your iPad or iPhone opened on the Mac looking unmarked: none of its ink, highlights, tape or cards were drawn, on any page. Your annotations had arrived and were stored safely the whole time; the Mac reader just never drew them. They now appear when the book opens.

Annotations that reach your Mac while a book is already open now appear straight away as well, instead of the next time you open it.
